    Privacy & Data Handling Policy

    SentinelCore Technologies is committed to maintaining strict data privacy, integrity, and confidentiality for all prospective clients and engaged partners.

    1. Information Collection

    When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect your name, business email address, organization name (if provided), and details regarding your technical requirements. We collect only what is strictly necessary to evaluate your inquiry.

    2. Use of Information

    Submitted information is used exclusively to evaluate your security and IT requirements, respond to inquiries, and prepare agreed scopes of work. We do not sell, rent, or distribute contact details to third-party marketing brokers.

    3. Confidentiality of Security Assessments

    All security consultations, website security reviews, and vulnerability assessments are conducted under strict mutual non-disclosure expectations. Any findings, system configurations, or vulnerability reports remain strictly confidential to your organization.

    4. Cookies & Tracking

    This static website does not utilize invasive tracking scripts, commercial ad pixels, or third-party behavioral profiling. Essential local storage may be used solely to remember user interface preferences.
